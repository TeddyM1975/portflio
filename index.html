01 — About

From raw data to autonomous systems.

I'm a data and AI professional with 6+ years of experience spanning the full stack of modern data work — analysis, machine learning, and the AI automation that puts insight into motion. I started in analytics, moved deep into data science and predictive modelling, and now spend most of my time designing AI and LLM-powered systems that run on their own.

Currently serving as AI Engineer at CircleHQ, I build agentic systems using n8n, Airtable, and automation pipelines that handle real business workflows end-to-end. At GlobalPay, I designed an Applicant Tracking System, automated financial reporting suites, and architected secure data pipelines connecting Power BI to AWS RDS PostgreSQL. I've shipped production-grade AI agents including a RAG-based Q&A bot, a WhatsApp notification system, and an OpenAI-powered travel chatbot — systems built for reliability, not just demos.

On the data science side, I've built and deployed end-to-end ML pipelines, NLP systems, and predictive models using Python, Scikit-learn, and Hugging Face Transformers — including a loan approval model that hit 91% accuracy. I'm equally comfortable writing advanced DAX, optimising SQL queries against large transaction datasets, or presenting findings to executive stakeholders.

My favourite problems sit where data meets automation: forecasting that triggers an action, a model that routes a ticket, an agent that drafts the report nobody wanted to write. I care about clarity, reliability, and shipping things people actually use.

I'm also the founder of Horizon Management Tech Solutions (H.M.T.), delivering affordable AI and automation solutions to schools and SMEs across Nigeria.
